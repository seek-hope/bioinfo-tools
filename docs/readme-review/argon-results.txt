Performance varies by workload: on 1M synthetic BAM reads with a 4-UMI adjacency graph, rust-umitools dedup reaches a 11.20x median paired speedup (0.453s vs 5.116s). On 1M real reads, cutadapt is near parity at 4 CPUs (0.779s vs 0.772s) and fq2fa is slightly faster (0.212s vs 0.270s), yet reverses at 10M synthetic reads (1.980s vs 1.289s). Currently, native stats, lint (829.4 vs 4.4 MiB peak RSS at 10M), flagstat, and sort run slower than upstream. rust-fastqc (9.306s vs 6.197s; 92.1 vs 322.9 MiB RSS) omits plots and differs in GC/duplication metrics, so no equal-work speedup or suite-wide average is claimed.中文 README 结果解释段落（168 字）正式基准覆盖 33 个测试条件（330 次计时运行另加 66 次预热），基于 aaf4ba4 加两项已存档修复补丁（stats Q20/Q30 与 fq2fa 换行修复），在非独占 i7-13700H 笔记本（tmpfs 读写、绑定 CPU 4 或 0,2,4,6、未锁频、配对间休眠 2 秒）上完成。性能随命令与输入而异：100 万合成 BAM（四 UMI 邻接图）下 umitools dedup 配对中位加速比为 11.20x（0.453s vs 5.116s）；100 万真实 reads 下 4 核 cutadapt 与上游持平（0.779s vs 0.772s），fq2fa 略快（0.212s vs 0.270s）但在千万合成 reads 下反慢于上游（1.980s vs 1.289s）。当前 stats、flagstat、sort 及 lint（千万级峰值 RSS 829.4 vs 4.4 MiB）均慢于上游。rust-fastqc（9.306s vs 6.197s，RSS 92.1 vs 322.9 MiB）仅校验基础计数、缺图形且存在 GC/重复度差异，故不计算等工作量加速比或全套件平均倍率。
